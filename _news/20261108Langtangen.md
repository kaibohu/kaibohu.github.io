I am honoured to receive the **2026 [Langtangen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hans_Petter_Langtangen) Prize** from the [Ridgway Scott Foundation](http://people.cs.uchicago.edu/~ridg/prizes/prizes.html), with [Ting Lin](https://alisomia.github.io) for our paper [“Finite element form-valued forms: Construction”](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.03243), recognised for “developing a unified construction of discretisations for tensor-valued functions”.
