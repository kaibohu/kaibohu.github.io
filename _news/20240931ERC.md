The project [GeoFEM (Geometric Finite Element Methods)](/geofem/) is funded by an [ERC Starting Grant](https://cordis.europa.eu/project/id/101164551). GeoFEM investigates the interactions between finite elements, geometric PDEs, discrete differential geometry and discrete physics.
